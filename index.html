Tabletop Games

APX

A Classless, Action Point Oriented, EXperience-Based TTRPG Engine

The Action Point Experience Engine is a tabletop roleplaying system built for high-stakes adventures. Whether you are exploring uncharted nebulas or delving into ancient dungeons, the game does not hand you survival in a package of preselected abilities. You earn your success through grit and tactical choices. Your survival depends entirely on how you spend your Action Points in the heat of the moment.
